Hussainabad's Broq.

Above every Balti village sits its broq — the high summer pasture where the livestock spends the season. Hussainabad's happens to come with stone stairs made for bike tricks, a night sky worth the climb, and Masrur Rock waiting at the top. Carry up, grin down.
